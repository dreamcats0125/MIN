# CM-2FA Protocol

Cerebro-Muscular Two-Factor Authentication

Version: 1.5.0

Status: Draft

Classification: Identity Verification Protocol

Authority Tier: Tier 2

---

# Purpose

CM-2FA defines a dual-channel identity verification architecture based on neural and muscular signals.

The protocol combines:

- EEG Brain Signals
- EMG Muscle Signals

to provide:

- Identity Verification
- Intent Validation
- Authorization Confirmation
- Coercion Detection
- Continuous Authentication

---

Replace passwords.

Reduce dependence on physical tokens.

Protect intent authenticity.

Preserve identity continuity.

Provide human-native authentication.

# Layer 1

## EMG Authentication Layer

Role:

Action Authorization Layer

Purpose:

Verify intentional motor commands.

---

### Verification Method

Micro-Gesture Recognition

and

Spatial Motion Signature Analysis

---

### Supported Signals

#### Micro Gesture Sequence

Examples:

- Finger Tap Patterns
- Invisible Muscle Activation
- Wrist Neural Impulses

Requirement:

No visible physical motion required.

Only valid neural-to-muscle activation is required.

---

#### Spatial Air Pen Trace

Purpose:

High-value authorization.

Measures:

- Velocity
- Acceleration
- Muscle Tension
- Motion Tensor Pattern

---

### Primary Objectives

- Authorization
- Gesture Verification
- High-Speed Confirmation
