# Definition

Omega Protocol is a proposed thermodynamic clearing protocol for an AI-dominated civilization.

Its purpose is not to create a product.

Its purpose is not to create a platform.

Its purpose is to establish physical boundaries for digital civilization.

---

# Core Observation

Digital civilization has escaped many traditional physical constraints.

Computation expands.

Models expand.

Content expands.

Financial systems expand.

---

However:

Energy remains finite.

Water remains finite.

Carbon capacity remains finite.

Thermodynamic limits remain finite.

---

# Problem Statement

Modern digital systems are capable of generating informational abundance while simultaneously increasing civilizational entropy.

Large-scale AI systems can produce unlimited content at marginal cost.

As content volume increases:

- Originality decreases
- Signal quality decreases
- Attention fragments
- Cultural density declines

---

Civilizations currently lack a universal mechanism for distinguishing entropy from value.

---

# Mission

Reconnect Digital Civilization to Physical Reality.

Reconnect Value Creation to Entropy Reduction.

Reconnect Computation to Ecological Limits.
