Logic Tear
Definition
Logic Tear (LT) is a conceptual unit representing crystallized insight produced through sustained reasoning and exploration.

Purpose
Logic Tear exists to preserve high-value conceptual discoveries.

Characteristics
Scarcity
Not every discussion creates a Logic Tear.

Traceability
Every Logic Tear should have a documented origin.

Preservation
Logic Tears are intended to remain as historical records.

Evolution
Logic Tears can inspire future concepts.
