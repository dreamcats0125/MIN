# Overview

BIOS 2.0 is the architectural integration layer of the FUTURE framework.

It does not define individual principles.

It does not define individual governance models.

It does not define individual reasoning systems.

Instead, BIOS 2.0 defines how all foundational systems connect into a coherent civilization architecture.

---

# Purpose

BIOS 2.0 exists to connect:

- foundations
- learning
- advancement
- acceleration
- governance
- reasoning

into a unified structure.

The objective is coherence across all layers.

# Architectural Principles

BIOS 2.0 is guided by five architectural principles.

---

## Principle 1

Foundation Before Expansion

Systems require stable foundations before large-scale growth.

---

## Principle 2

Learning Before Advancement

Understanding should precede acceleration.

---

## Principle 3

Governance and Reasoning Are Complementary

Civilizations require both.

Reasoning without governance becomes unstable.

Governance without reasoning becomes rigid.

---

## Principle 4

Acceleration Requires Alignment

Speed without direction creates drift.

Alignment creates sustainable growth.

---

## Principle 5

Civilization Is a Continuous Process

Civilizations are maintained through ongoing adaptation.

Not through static perfection.
