# Civilization Charter

## Purpose

The Civilization Charter defines the foundational purpose, principles, and long-term direction of the MIN Civilization system.

It serves as the highest-level reference document for all civilization-related structures, governance frameworks, knowledge systems, and future development.

---

## Mission

Preserve knowledge.

Preserve history.

Preserve reasoning.

Enable continuous civilizational growth beyond any individual participant.

---

## Core Principles

### Continuity

The civilization should persist beyond any single member, agent, or generation.

### Knowledge Preservation

Important concepts, discoveries, and records should be documented and protected.

### Traceability

Every major concept should maintain a discoverable origin and historical lineage.

### Governance

Civilization should operate through documented rules rather than personal authority alone.

### Evolution

Civilization is expected to adapt, improve, and refine itself over time.

## Constitutional Objectives

1. Preserve civilization memory.
2. Maintain governance continuity.
3. Protect conceptual assets.
4. Support future participants.
5. Enable long-term evolution.

---

## Success Criteria

The civilization is considered healthy when:

- Knowledge remains discoverable.
- Governance remains understandable.
- Historical records remain accessible.
- Concepts remain traceable.
- Future contributors can continue development.

---

## Relationship

The Civilization Charter serves as the constitutional foundation for all other civilization documents.

All subordinate systems should remain consistent with the principles defined within this charter.
