# Crystal Master Registry

## Purpose

The Crystal Master Registry serves as the authoritative index of all recognized crystals within the MIN Civilization system.

It provides a centralized reference for crystal status, lineage, and historical preservation.

---

## Mission

Maintain a complete crystal inventory.

Provide discoverability.

Support traceability.

Preserve civilizational memory.

---

## Registry Principles

### Single Source of Reference

Every recognized crystal should appear within the Master Registry.

### Traceable Lineage

Every crystal should maintain links to its origin and evolution.

### Status Transparency

The lifecycle status of each crystal should remain visible.

### Historical Preservation

Retired crystals remain part of civilization history.

---

## Crystal Status Categories

### Emerging

Newly identified concept.

### Registered

Officially recorded.

### Validated

Reviewed and refined.

### Elevated

Recognized as a durable civilizational asset.

### Legacy

Historically significant.

### Cemetery

Retired and preserved.

- The concept has defined relationships.
- Traceability can be maintained.
