# Three House Governance

## Purpose

The Three House Governance model defines the primary governance structure of the MIN Civilization system.

It distributes responsibility across multiple houses to reduce dependence on any single individual, role, or institution.

---

## Mission

Balance authority.

Preserve continuity.

Maintain accountability.

Support long-term civilizational development.

---

## Principles

### Separation of Responsibilities

Different governance functions should be distributed across specialized houses.

### Mutual Oversight

Each house should provide checks and balances for the others.

### Continuity

The governance model should survive leadership transitions.

### Knowledge Preservation

Governance decisions should remain traceable and documented.

## Decision Model

Major decisions should consider perspectives from all three houses.

Governance House:
"What is permitted?"

Knowledge House:
"What should be preserved?"

Development House:
"What should be created next?"

---

## Success Criteria

The Three House Governance model is considered healthy when:

- Governance remains stable.
- Knowledge remains preserved.
- Development remains active.
- No single house dominates the entire system.
- Civilizational continuity is maintained.

---

## Relationship

Three House Governance operates beneath the Civilization Charter and provides the primary governance framework for coordination between civilization domains.
