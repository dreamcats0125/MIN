# Executive Summary

RTCP (Real-Time Compute Protocol) is a civilization-scale protocol that treats ecological capacity as the source of monetary scarcity, computation as economic cost, and entropy reduction as economic value.

The protocol proposes a future monetary system designed for an AI-dominated civilization.

Instead of relying on political credibility, financial leverage, or artificial scarcity, RTCP anchors digital civilization to physical reality.

---

# Mission

Anchor Computation to Ecology.

Anchor Value to Entropy Reduction.

Anchor Civilization to Physics.

---

# Core Premise

The fundamental scarce resource of the AI era is not information.

The fundamental scarce resource is sustainable computation.

Computation consumes:

- Energy
- Water
- Carbon Capacity
- Heat Capacity
- Ecological Carrying Capacity

Therefore:

Computation is a physical asset.

---

# Civilizational Problem

Modern digital systems can generate unlimited content.

Physical systems cannot provide unlimited resources.

This creates a growing divergence between:

Digital Expansion

and

Physical Reality

---

RTCP attempts to reconnect those two worlds.

ComputeCap defines the maximum allowable computational budget of civilization.

It functions as a constitutional limit.

## Objective

- Resource Monopolization
