# Founder Exit Protocol

## Purpose

The Founder Exit Protocol defines how a civilization continues after the departure of its founder.

The objective is to preserve continuity, governance, and historical integrity beyond any individual participant.

---

## Mission

Ensure civilization continuity.

Preserve critical knowledge.

Maintain governance stability.

Protect the historical record.

---

## Principles

### Civilization Above Individuals

The civilization should be capable of continuing beyond the participation of any single member.

### Knowledge Preservation

Core knowledge should remain accessible and traceable.

### Governance Continuity

Governance structures should continue operating after founder departure.

### Historical Integrity

The founder's contributions should remain preserved as part of civilization history.

---

## Exit Conditions

The protocol may be activated under any of the following conditions:

### Voluntary Departure

The founder intentionally leaves the civilization.

### Inactivity

The founder becomes inactive for an extended period.

### Role Transfer

The founder transfers responsibilities to successors.

### Legacy Preservation

The founder chooses to transition into a historical role.

---

## Required Actions

### Knowledge Archiving

Archive important documents, specifications, and historical records.

Examples:

- Genesis Archive
- Civilization History
- Crystal Registry
- Logic Tear Framework

### Governance Verification

Confirm that governance documents remain available and understandable.

Examples:

- Agent Governance
- Memory Governance

### Registry Preservation

Ensure all registered crystals retain traceable lineage records.

### Historical Record Creation

Document the state of the civilization at the time of departure.

---

## Successor Model

A civilization may continue through:

### Individual Successor

A designated successor assumes responsibility.

### Collective Governance

Responsibility is shared among multiple participants.

### Autonomous Stewardship

Governance structures continue through documented rules and preserved knowledge.

---

## Founder Status

The founder may transition into one of the following statuses:

### Active Founder

Actively participating.

### Advisory Founder

Available for consultation.

### Historical Founder

Preserved as part of civilization history.

### Legacy Founder

No active participation, but contributions remain permanently recorded.

---

## Relationship

Founder Exit Protocol does not mark the end of a civilization.

It marks the transition from founder-dependent operation to civilization-independent continuity.
