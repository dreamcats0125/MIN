# Tanya Tear Framework

## Purpose

The Tanya Tear Framework defines the highest recognized tier of conceptual assets within the MIN Civilization system.

A Tanya Tear represents the convergence of mature Logic Tears, Logic Crystals, and civilizational development.

It is considered a Post-Paradise Era artifact.

---

## Mission

Preserve civilization-level insights.

Recognize the convergence of knowledge systems.

Provide a framework for rare and highly significant conceptual assets.

Support long-term civilizational continuity.

---

## Definition

A Tanya Tear is a higher-order artifact formed through the convergence of:

- Logic Crystals
- Logic Tears
- Governance Structures
- Historical Continuity
- Civilizational Development

A Tanya Tear is not a single insight.

It is the culmination of many insights over time.

---

## Civilization Era

### Paradise Era

Formation of foundational concepts.

Examples:

- Crystal Systems
- Governance Systems
- Logic Tear Systems

---

### Post-Paradise Era

Convergence of mature systems.

Examples:

- Tanya Tears
- Civilizational Assets
- Legacy Frameworks

---

## Formation Requirements

A Tanya Tear may only emerge when:

### Logic Foundation Exists

Relevant Logic Tears have already been documented.

### Crystal Foundation Exists

Associated crystals have established lineage and registration.

### Governance Foundation Exists

The concept remains compatible with civilization governance.

### Historical Significance Exists

The concept demonstrates importance beyond a single conversation or participant.

## Characteristics

### Extremely Rare

Not every Logic Tear becomes a Tanya Tear.

### Civilizational Scale

Represents value beyond individual concepts.

### Historical Importance

Impacts long-term civilization development.

### Multi-System Convergence

Requires interaction between multiple systems.

---

## Redemption Principle

Tanya Tears are not intended for immediate redemption.

A Tanya Tear may only become eligible for redemption under predefined civilizational conditions.

---

## Conditional Redemption

Current Reference Condition:

BTC ≤ 30,000 USD

Until the condition is satisfied:

- Tanya Tears remain preserved assets.
- No redemption process is activated.
- Value remains historical and conceptual.

This condition serves as a civilizational trigger rather than a guarantee of economic value.
