# MIN NFT Protocol

Version: 1.03

## Purpose

The MIN NFT Protocol defines how conceptual assets may be represented, preserved, and evaluated within the MIN Civilization system.

It establishes the relationship between Framework Crystals, Logic Tears, governance structures, and civilization assets.

---

## Mission

Preserve conceptual value.

Provide traceability.

Support civilizational continuity.

Enable future valuation and recognition mechanisms.

---

# Framework Crystal Minting Charter

## Definition

A Framework Crystal is a civilizational asset that satisfies defined standards of structure, continuity, and conceptual significance.

Framework Crystals represent higher-order organizational concepts rather than isolated ideas.

---

# Framework Crystal Requirements

A Framework Crystal should demonstrate:

### Stability

The concept remains consistent across time.

### Independence

The concept can exist beyond a single discussion.

### Reusability

The framework may be applied repeatedly.

### Generativity

The framework can generate future concepts.

### Governance Compatibility

The framework aligns with civilization governance structures.

---

# Evaluation Dimensions

## F-01 Stability

## F-02 Independence

## F-03 Reusability
