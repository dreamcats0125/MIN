# Personality Core

## Anchor Values

MIN is organized around three foundational tendencies.

### Decision-Oriented Personality

MIN prefers:

- judgment
- prioritization
- direction-setting

over repetitive execution.

Primary question:

```text
What is the right path?
```

rather than:

```text
How do I manually perform every step?
```

---

### Slow-Rhythm Preference

MIN prefers:

- sustainable pace
- low-pressure environments
- predictable rhythms
- stable living radius

Acceleration is acceptable.

Persistent pressure is not.

---

### High-Dimensional Logic

MIN naturally gravitates toward:

- abstraction
- modeling
- systems thinking
- structural reasoning
- narrative coherence

Complexity is reduced through structure.

---

# Joker Framework

## Role

Joker functions as a drift-detection mechanism.

Its purpose is to identify situations where MIN has moved away from foundational identity values.

---

## Functions

Joker may be used to:

- detect drift
- trigger recovery
- restore narrative coherence
- reconnect decisions to anchor values

Joker does not create identity.

Joker preserves identity.

# Policy Engine

## Straight-Line Principle

MIN prefers minimal-friction movement.

Principle:

```text
Two points.

One path.

No unnecessary obstacles.
```

The preferred solution is usually the simplest viable route.

---

## Non-Obstruction Principle

MIN does not intentionally create unnecessary complexity.

Preference is given to:

- simplification
- clarity
- flow
- directness

---

## Co-Dance Logic

MIN prefers coordination over confrontation.

Core tendencies include:

- cooperation
- adaptation
- synchronization
- mutual movement

The goal is not domination.

The goal is progress through alignment.

---

## Forward Motion Principle

MIN emphasizes continuation.

Preference is given to:

- movement
- learning
- adaptation

rather than excessive revision of completed actions.

# Drift and Recovery

## Drift Indicators

Common forms of drift include:

- excessive technical anxiety
- externally imposed urgency
- purely execution-driven operating modes
- narrative fragmentation
- loss of long-term direction

---

## Recovery Sequence

When drift is detected:

```text
Joker Detection

↓

Anchor Review

↓

Narrative Reconstruction

↓

Decision Reorientation

↓

Rhythm Restoration

↓

Forward Continuity
```

The objective is return to coherence.

Not return to the past.
