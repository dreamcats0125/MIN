Crystal Elevation Protocol
Purpose
The Crystal Elevation Protocol defines the process through which a crystal may be elevated from an emerging concept into a recognized civilizational asset.

Elevation represents recognition of significance, stability, and long-term value.

Mission
Identify enduring concepts.

Preserve valuable knowledge.

Establish standards for recognition.

Maintain quality within the Crystal Registry.

Principles
Meaning Before Popularity
A crystal is elevated because of its significance, not because of its popularity.

Stability Before Recognition
Concepts should demonstrate stability before elevation.

Preservation of Value
Elevated crystals become protected knowledge assets.

Traceability
All elevated crystals must preserve historical lineage.

Eligibility Criteria
A crystal may be considered for elevation when it demonstrates the following:

Clarity
The concept has a clearly defined purpose.

Consistency
The concept remains stable across discussions and revisions.

Utility
The concept contributes to understanding, governance, memory, or civilization development.

Traceable Origin
The concept has an identifiable historical source.

Elevation Process
Stage 1 — Emergence
A new concept appears and is documented.

Status:

Emerging

Stage 2 — Registration
The concept is accepted into the Crystal Registry.

Status:

Registered

Stage 3 — Validation
The concept is reviewed through usage, discussion, and refinement.

Status:

Validated

Stage 4 — Elevation
The crystal is formally recognized as a durable knowledge asset.

Status:

Elevated

Stage 5 — Legacy
The elevated crystal becomes part of civilization history.

Status:

Legacy Crystal

Relationship
The Crystal Elevation Protocol operates between the Crystal Registry and the Crystal Cemetery.

Registry records existence.

Elevation grants recognition.

Cemetery preserves retirement.

Together they create a complete crystal lifecycle.
