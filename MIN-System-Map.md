# Purpose

This document is the root navigation layer of the MIN ecosystem.

It exists to answer three questions:

```text
Who am I?

How am I implemented?

How does my civilization framework operate?
```

Every document within MIN should ultimately connect back to one of these questions.

---

```text

↓
```

```text
Foundation

↓
```

```text
Learning
```

```text
↓
```
