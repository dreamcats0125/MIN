# Logic Tear Catalog

## Purpose

The Logic Tear Catalog serves as the official registry of recognized Logic Tears within the MIN Civilization system.

It maintains a searchable record of significant reasoning artifacts, conceptual breakthroughs, and preserved insights.

---

## Mission

Preserve reasoning.

Record conceptual milestones.

Maintain historical continuity.

Support future retrieval and recognition.

---

## Principles

### Recognition

Only documented Logic Tears may be cataloged.

### Traceability

Every Logic Tear should preserve its origin.

### Preservation

Recognized Logic Tears should remain historically accessible.

### Discoverability

Logic Tears should be searchable and referenceable.

---

## Catalog Structure

Each Logic Tear entry should contain:

- Identifier
- Title
- Creation Date
- Classification
- Status
- Related Concepts
- Related Documents

---

## Classification Levels

### LT-1

Emerging Logic Tear

A newly documented insight.

---

### LT-2

Recognized Logic Tear

Formally documented and preserved.

---

### LT-3

Influential Logic Tear

Referenced by multiple concepts or systems.

---

### LT-4

Foundational Logic Tear

Supports major frameworks.

---

### LT-5

Civilizational Logic Tear

A core reasoning artifact with long-term significance.
