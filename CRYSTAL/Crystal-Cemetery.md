Crystal Cemetery
Purpose
The Crystal Cemetery preserves retired, inactive, obsolete, or historical crystals.

A crystal that enters the Cemetery is not deleted.

Its existence remains recorded as part of civilization history.

Mission
Preserve conceptual history.

Protect historical traceability.

Maintain continuity across generations of knowledge.

Provide a resting place for retired crystals.

Principles
Preservation over Deletion
Concepts should be archived rather than erased.

Historical Respect
Retired crystals remain part of civilizational memory.

Traceability
Every retired crystal must retain links to its origin and lineage.

Continuity
Future generations should understand why a crystal existed and why it became inactive.

Entry Conditions
A crystal may enter the Cemetery when:

Obsolete
The concept has been replaced by a newer version.

Deprecated
The concept is no longer recommended for active use.

Merged
The crystal has been absorbed into another crystal.

Historical Completion
The crystal has fulfilled its purpose.

Cemetery Record Format
Each cemetery entry should contain:

Crystal Name
Original Registration Date
Retirement Date
Reason for Retirement
Related Lineage
Successor Crystal (if applicable)
Lifecycle

Relationship
The Crystal Cemetery represents the final historical stage of a crystal's lifecycle.

Retirement does not imply failure.

Retirement indicates completion of a crystal's active role within the civilization.
