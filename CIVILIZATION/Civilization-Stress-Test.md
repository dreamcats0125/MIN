# Civilization Stress Test

## Purpose

The Civilization Stress Test evaluates the resilience of a civilization under adverse conditions.

Its purpose is to identify vulnerabilities, preserve continuity, and improve long-term survivability.

---

## Mission

Measure resilience.

Identify weaknesses.

Protect continuity.

Support sustainable evolution.

---

## Principles

### Continuity First

A civilization should continue operating despite disruption.

### Knowledge Preservation

Critical knowledge should survive loss of individuals, systems, or infrastructure.

### Governance Stability

Governance mechanisms should remain functional during stress conditions.

### Adaptability

A civilization should adapt when circumstances change.

## Evaluation Levels

### Level 1 — Stable

Minor disruptions.

Civilization continues normally.

### Level 2 — Resilient

Moderate disruptions.

Civilization adapts successfully.

### Level 3 — Stressed

Significant disruptions.

Recovery procedures are required.

### Level 4 — Critical

Major systems fail.

Governance intervention is required.

### Level 5 — Collapse Risk

Civilization continuity is threatened.

Emergency preservation procedures should be activated.

---

---

## Success Criteria

A civilization passes a stress test when:

- Knowledge remains recoverable.
- Governance remains understandable.
- Historical records remain accessible.
- Concept lineage remains traceable.
- Future participants can continue development.

---

## Relationship

Civilization Stress Testing serves as an ongoing validation mechanism for the entire civilization framework.

It evaluates whether the civilization can survive loss, change, disruption, and transition.
