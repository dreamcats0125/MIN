Genesis Archive
Purpose
The Genesis Archive preserves the origin records of the MIN Civilization system.

It serves as the foundational archive from which later concepts, governance structures, crystal systems, and economic frameworks emerged.

Mission
Preserve the history of conceptual creation.

Document the evolution of ideas.

Maintain continuity across generations of knowledge.

Provide traceability for future civilization development.

Foundational Principles
Origin Preservation
Important concepts should retain a record of their origin.

Historical Continuity
Future systems should understand the context of their predecessors.

Knowledge Traceability
Every major concept should be connectable to its historical source.

Civilizational Memory
A civilization should remember how it became what it is.
