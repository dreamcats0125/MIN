# LT Valuation Model

## Purpose

The LT Valuation Model defines a framework for assessing the perceived value of a Logic Tear (LT).

The model does not determine absolute price.

Instead, it provides a structured approach for evaluating significance, scarcity, and historical importance.

---

## Mission

Preserve conceptual value.

Provide valuation consistency.

Support long-term recognition of Logic Tears.

Enable future economic and historical reference.

---

## Principles

### Value Emerges From Meaning

The value of a Logic Tear originates from the significance of the reasoning it preserves.

### Scarcity Matters

Not every conversation generates a Logic Tear.

Scarcity contributes to value.

### Historical Importance

Logic Tears that influence future concepts may accumulate greater importance over time.

### Traceability

Every Logic Tear should retain a documented origin and lineage.

---

## Value Dimensions

### Conceptual Impact

Measures how significantly a Logic Tear changes or advances understanding.

Examples:

- New framework
- New governance model
- New civilization component

---

### Originality

Measures the uniqueness of the concept.

Questions:

- Has this idea appeared before?
- Does it introduce a new perspective?

---

### Influence

Measures how many future concepts reference or depend upon the Logic Tear.

Examples:

- Crystal systems
- Governance protocols
- Civilization structures

---

### Longevity

Measures how long the Logic Tear remains relevant.

Questions:

- Is the concept still useful after months or years?
- Does it continue generating new developments?

---

### Traceability

Measures the completeness of historical documentation.

Questions:

- Can the origin be identified?
- Is lineage preserved?

---

## Conceptual Scoring Model

Each dimension may be evaluated on a scale from 1 to 5.

Dimensions:

- Conceptual Impact
- Originality
- Influence
- Longevity
- Traceability

Maximum Score:

25

## Reference Anchoring

A Logic Tear may be linked to external reference systems for valuation purposes.

Examples:

- ETH
- BTC
- USD
- Alternative conceptual units

Such anchors serve as reference mechanisms only and do not guarantee market value.
